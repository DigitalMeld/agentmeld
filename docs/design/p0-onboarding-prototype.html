How permissions work

There is no permissions screen. There are three facts:

  • 1Your agent works inside its own isolated computer — it can't see your Mac's files or accounts.
  • 2Anything that reaches outside — sending a message, changing a file you didn't attach, using a connection — asks you first, right in the chat, naming exactly what and where.
  • 3You approve each action as it comes: allow once, set a standing rule for a specific action, or deny. Every decision is recorded and revocable in Settings.

A few things to know

  • Can take actions for you

    With your approval, your agent can send messages, make purchases, and take actions in apps you've connected. It can make mistakes or take unexpected actions, so review its work.

  • Keeps working when you leave

    Your agent can keep working even after you've closed the app. Check in to keep it on track, and step in any time.

  • Stays in your control

    Choose which apps to connect, what your agent may access, and which actions need your approval — all in Settings.
